# Hebrew for teamod: one word for each thing

Every shard uses these. Short, plain Israeli UI Hebrew, the way a good Hebrew app reads.

- **Gender-neutral.** Buttons are nouns of action (שמירה, מיזוג, הוספת פרויקט), never a gendered
  imperative (הוסף/הוסיפי). When the text must address the person, use plural (בחרו, לחצו).
- **Short.** A pane is 60–90 columns. A button is one to three words.
- **Never translated:** people's and projects' names, paths, branch names, code, commands, model
  names (Claude, Opus), product names (GitHub, Linear, Cloudflare, teamod), hotkey letters,
  and the words "PR", "Issue", "Actions" (GitHub's own).
- Numbers stay digits; times are short: `{#0}m` → `{0} ד׳`, `{#0}h` → `{0} ש׳`, `{#0}d` → `{0} י׳`.
